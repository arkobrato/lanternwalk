# Lanternwalk

A calm twenty-minute adventure game with no way to lose. Carry a lantern from morning to nightfall, gather the small lights along the path, and stop for the nineteen people from around the world who are glad to see you. Each one gives you a keepsake you can see on your traveler or use from the tray at the bottom of the screen.

## Play

Download `index.html` and open it in any browser. The whole game is in that one file.

- Walk: arrow keys or A / D. On a phone, hold the on-screen arrows.
- Talk: the button that appears near each person, or Enter.
- Keepsakes: tap one in the tray to put it in your purse, and tap it again to take it out.
- A soft melody plays in the background. Sound can be switched off in the top corner.
